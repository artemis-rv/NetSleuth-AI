## Complete Phase-by-Phase Breakdown

### Phase 1: M4 Evidence Boundary & ReportEngine
- **Location**: `backend/app/engines/reporting/report_engine.py`
- **Purpose**: The central orchestrator that isolates M4 from M1/M2/M3 internals. It accepts serialized `InvestigationCase` payloads and delegates parsing, integrity checking, and rendering.
- **Constraint Enforcement**: Strictly read-only. It NEVER mutates raw intelligence, invents causality, or alters IDs/hashes.

---

### Phase 2: Case Adapter & Version Negotiation
- **Location**: `backend/app/engines/reporting/case_adapter.py`
- **Purpose**: Dynamically identifies the incoming schema (`V1.1` vs `V1.2`). 
- **Backward Compatibility**: Fully backward compatible. It seamlessly translates legacy V1.1 payloads while natively extracting rich MITRE mappings, Attack Chains, and timeline data from newer V1.2 cases.

---

### Phase 3: Cryptographic Integrity & Chain of Custody
- **Location**: `backend/app/engines/reporting/integrity_verifier.py`, `backend/app/engines/reporting/chain_of_custody.py`
- **Key Artifacts**: `EvidencePackage`, `Evidence Integrity Contract`.
- **Purpose**: 
  - Verifies hashes (`SHA-256`) of raw capture files, logs, and artifacts against original acquisition records.
  - Builds an immutable `ChainOfCustody` log preserving the exact chronological lineage from `NetworkIntelligencePackage` to `InvestigationCase`.

---

### Phase 4: Frozen Report Contracts & JSON Exporter
- **Location**: `docs/contracts/report-v1.json`, `docs/contracts/report-v1.1.json`, `backend/app/engines/reporting/report_exporter.py`
- **Contracts**: Frozen and immutable. V1.1 adds robust structural support for MITRE semantics and presentation.
- **JSON Exporter**: Dumps the fully assembled Report object into strict, schema-validated JSON format for external machine consumption.

---

### Phase 5 & 6: HTML and PDF Presentation Renderers
- **Location**: `backend/app/engines/reporting/html_renderer.py`, `backend/app/engines/reporting/pdf_renderer.py`
- **Purpose**: Transforms the highly technical Report JSON into human-readable, executive-ready visual formats.
- **MITRE Preservation**: Explicitly updated to natively parse and elegantly render `mitre_mappings` and `attack_chain` intelligence generated by M3. 
- **Data Loss**: NO. All analytical fidelity is preserved perfectly into the final visualizations.
